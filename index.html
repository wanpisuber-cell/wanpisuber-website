Port Moresby Travel Information

For Our Local Guests

Payment flexibility: BSP mobile banking, cash, or card in our premium vehicles.

Service area: All NCD suburbs including Waigani, Gordons, Boroko, Town, and beyond.

Local rates: Competitive pricing starting from PGK 18 within the city zone.

International Visitor Guide

Arriving at POM: Jacksons International Airport. Our driver meets you at arrivals.

Currency: Papua New Guinean Kina (PGK). Our app displays approximate USD fares.

Connectivity: Digicel or Bmobile SIM available at the airport. English widely spoken.

Safety tip: We recommend pre-booking all transport. All our rides are tracked and insured.
